# No money now, but a concrete plan to get it

A money block only means **No Potential** when there is no route back. When the same
conversation names a concrete near-term source of funds the lead stays **Call Again**:

- salary: `will get my salary in few days`, `until salary time then she will start`
- arranging: `will try to arrange the funds`, `will bring it then continue`
- other people: `let me ask my friends`, `will get help from someone and deposit`
- a pinned time: `callback next week she will get the funds`, `cb in the evening`

It stays **No Potential** when the timing is unknown (`not sure when he can get money`),
distant (`in 4 months`, `by October`, `save up`), or the lead admits they were never
serious (`i was actually not very serious`).
