# No Interest needs two refusal days

A single refusal or non-cooperative call (`playing around`, `didn't give me time to
talk`, `hung up after intro`) is a **Recall**. **No Interest** requires refusal-level
utterances on two different days.

A bare hang-up on its own is not a refusal at all: `hu` closes almost every note in
these logs, including plain no-answer ones.
